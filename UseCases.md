# Interoperability analysis - Use Cases
As part of a comprehansive assessment of data integration challenges, we have identified use case within the carbonate system across different regions and observational contexts. These use cases will help assess the feasibility and effectiveness of data sharing and also aid in evaluating the challenges and potential solutions for seamless data exchange in oceanographic research.

### Use Cases:
1. **Fixed-Location Stakeholder Data Integration**  
This use case focuses on integrating data from various stakeholders at fixed locations over a defined time period.  

    Deployments planned within GEORGE project that qualify:  
    1. Norwegian Sea: Station M  
        - Location: Norwegian Sea, Station M  
        - Objective/Goal: Measure pCO2 in deep-sea environment, in-mooring behaviour  
        
    2. Norwegian Sea at 300: EMSO Mohn  
        - Location: Norwegian Sea, EMSO Mohn
        - Objective/Goal: Measure pCO2 and CH4 in a deep-sea environment, seabed behaviour

2. **Moving-Location Stakeholder Data Integration**  
This use case focuses on integrating data from various stakeholders at moving locations over a defined time period.

    Deployments planned within GEORGE project that qualify:
    1. Platform on a ship of opportunity in the Baltic Sea  
        - Location: the Baltic Sea, Finnmaid Vessel  
        - Objective/Goal: Evaluation of sensors developed in WP2 and COTS sensors and their ability to characterize the carbonate system in coastal Baltic Sea across a range of salinities.  
    2. Platform on a ship of opportunity in the North Atlantic  
        - Location: North Atlantic, Atlantic Sail Vessel 
        - Objective/Goal: Evaluation of the sensors developed in WP2 to characterize the carbonate system.  

3. **Glider Data Merging for Adaptive Sampling**  
This use case focuses on combining data from multiple gliders, which is relevant in light of upcoming advancements in adaptive sampling.  

    Deployments planned within GEORGE project that qualify:
    1. Deployment of opportunity in the Ligurian Sea  
        - Location: Ligurian Sea, Gliders and Argo Floats
        - Objective/Goal: Deploy and test over a few days including various sensors developed or improved WP2 on mobile platforms.
